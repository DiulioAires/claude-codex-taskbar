# Provider logos

The Claude asterisk and Codex mark are sourced from LobeHub's `lobe-icons`
(MIT), retrieved on 2026-11-11:

- https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/claude.svg
- https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/codex.svg

The unmodified SVG source files are kept beside this notice. The renderer embeds
flattened contours from these files (24-unit viewBox, 0.035-unit tolerance),
scaled uniformly and painted with the current theme color. The completion pulse
adds a background behind the logo only. No assets are fetched at runtime.

Claude and its mark belong to Anthropic. Codex and its mark belong to OpenAI.
